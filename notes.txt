Returns parsed components as a JSON object, or individual fields

Ships static USPS suffix and state abbreviation tables — no
network calls, no external data files at runtime.
US-only for v0.0.1; international forms are out of scope.

::note When parsing the Delivery Address Line into the individual components, start from the right–most element of the address and work toward the left. 

::note Exceptions are any combinations of NORTH-SOUTH or EAST–WEST as consecutive words. In these cases the second directional becomes part of the street name and is spelled out completely in the street name field.

::note When parsing from right to left, if the directional word appears between the street name and the suffix, then it appears as part of the street name spelled out in the ZIP+4 file and is spelled out on the mailpiece.

::note If an address has two consecutive words that appear on the suffix table (Appendix C), abbreviate the second of the two words according to the suffix table and place it in the suffix field. The first of the two words is part of the street name. Spell it out on the mailpiece in its entirety after the street name.

address ::= deliver_line  last_line

delivery_line ::= house_num 
    [pre_directional]
    street_name
    [street_suffix] // this becomes part of the street name
    [street_suffix]
    [consecutive_directional] // this become part of street name
    [post_directional]
    [address_unit]

street_name ::= [consecutive_directional]
    word 
    [directional]

pre_directional ::= directional {directional} 
post_directional ::= directional {directional}
two_directional ::= directional directional
address_unit ::= unit_designator unit_identifier


<directional> := directionals
<street_suffix> := suffixes
<secondary_address> := alphanumeric


last_line ::= city state zip

<city> := word {word}
<state> := states
<zip>  := zip5 [ "-" zip4 ]
